FEATURES

Why Choose Me

Work Experience

Hi, I’m Irine. I’m a professional Makeup Artist and Hairstylist with 18 years of experience in makeup and 8 years in hairstyling. Bridal beauty is truly my passion. I love creating soft, romantic, elegant looks that enhance your natural beauty and still feel like you.

Introduction

There is something so special about being part of a bride’s wedding morning. I love the excitement, the happy emotions, and that beautiful moment when she sees her finished look for the first time. For me, beauty is about more than makeup and hair — it’s about making you feel beautiful, comfortable, and cared for. I bring my experience, creativity, and attention to every detail to create a look that feels effortless, romantic, and uniquely yours.
